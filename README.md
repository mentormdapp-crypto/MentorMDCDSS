# MentorMDCDSS
Clinical Decision Support System 

MentorMD is an independently developed clinical intelligence and medical education platform designed to address an important gap in healthcare technology: the fragmentation of clinical reasoning, medical knowledge and patient assessment across disconnected tools.

I founded MentorMD based on my experience as a medical doctor working in clinical environments, particularly emergency medicine and intensive care.

The project aims to bring multiple clinical capabilities into one integrated environment, including AI-assisted clinical reasoning, differential diagnosis generation, ECG interpretation, ABG analysis, laboratory report analysis, medical imaging interpretation, medication safety, dosage calculations and evidence-based clinical protocols.

A central focus of MentorMD is developing a continuously evolving clinical reasoning engine. Unlike conventional applications that generate a static response from an initial presentation, MentorMD is designed to reassess its reasoning as new symptoms, examination findings, vital signs and investigation results become available.

The project addresses a particularly important challenge in resource-constrained healthcare environments: making structured clinical knowledge and advanced medical education tools more accessible.

MentorMD is currently in its early development stage. It does not yet have substantial public adoption, external contributors or measurable download statistics. I am establishing its public open-source development foundation while continuing to develop its architecture and core functionality.

My long-term objective is to build an extensible clinical intelligence platform that can evolve through contributions from software developers, medical professionals and researchers internationally.

I believe the intersection of artificial intelligence and clinical medicine offers significant opportunities, but building trustworthy healthcare technology requires rigorous engineering, clinical validation and continuous improvement.

I intend to use Claude Max 20x as a major engineering resource throughout the development of MentorMD.

The project involves complex software engineering challenges across clinical reasoning architecture, AI orchestration, backend infrastructure, database design, medical knowledge integration and clinical safety validation.

My primary intended uses include:

1. Clinical reasoning engine development: Designing and implementing MentorMD’s multi-stage reasoning architecture, including differential diagnosis generation, evidence integration and reassessment when new clinical information becomes available.
2. Backend engineering: Developing and refining the Supabase backend, database architecture, edge functions, API integrations and supporting infrastructure.
3. AI engineering: Building reliable AI workflows, improving model orchestration, structured outputs, context management and reasoning pipelines.
4. Clinical safety engineering: Conducting systematic architecture reviews, identifying edge cases, developing validation scenarios and testing the reliability of clinical workflows. Clinical outputs will remain subject to independent medical review.
5. Claude Code development workflow: Using Claude Code for implementation, debugging, refactoring, test development and codebase-wide engineering tasks.
6. Open-source readiness: Improving documentation, developer onboarding, contribution guidelines and code maintainability to facilitate future contributions from the wider developer community.
